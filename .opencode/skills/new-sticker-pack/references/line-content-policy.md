# LINE Creator Market — Content Policy Reference

## Guideline 3.13: Prohibited Religious & Sensitive Content

The following themes cause **automatic rejection** by LINE moderation.

### Banned keywords (case-insensitive)
```
jesus, christ, mary, cross, faith, peace, angel, bible, god,
prayer, pray, holy, church, mosque, temple, allah, buddha,
krishna, shiva, worship, sermon, gospel, hymn, baptism,
confirmation, communion, crusade, cathedral, shrine
```

### Additional prohibited categories
- Political figures, parties, or slogans
- Brand logos or copyrighted mascots (Mickey Mouse, Pikachu, etc.)
- Explicit violence or gore
- Sexual or nudity content
- Gambling references (dice, poker chips, casino imagery)
- Alcohol or tobacco depicted as appealing to minors

## Allowed Religious Adjacent Content
- Generic stars, hearts, sparkles
- "Love", "hope" without religious framing
- Seasonal imagery (Christmas tree without cross, generic gifts)

## Sticker Count Rules
Valid counts: **8, 16, 24, 32, 40** — any other count will fail at submission.

## Image Specifications
| Asset | Size | Format | Max size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sticker | 370 × 320 px | PNG | 1 MB |
| Main image | 240 × 240 px | PNG | 1 MB |
| Tab icon | 96 × 74 px | PNG | 1 MB |

## Title & Description Limits
- Title: max 40 characters
- Description: max 160 characters
- No special characters: `< > & " '`
