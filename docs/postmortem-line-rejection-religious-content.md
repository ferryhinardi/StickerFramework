# Post-Mortem: LINE Creators Market Rejections - Rule 3.13 (Religious Content)

**Date:** 2026-02-23
**Status:** Complete
**Rejection Rule:** 3.13 - Images designed to solicit or spread religion, or with strong religious components

---

## Summary

Four sticker packs were rejected by LINE Creators Market review under guideline 3.13 (religious content). This document analyzes each rejected image to understand what triggered the rejection and proposes corrective actions.

## Detailed Analysis

### 1. boba-milo-3 - Image 01 (`01_miss_you`)

**Image Description:** Two cute otters (Boba & Milo) hugging each other with hearts around them. Text reads "Miss you". One otter wears a teal scarf, the other wears glasses.

**Visual Analysis:**
- No religious text, symbols, or iconography visible
- The pose shows two characters hugging (arms wrapped around each other)
- Hearts and romantic/affectionate theme only

**Possible Rejection Reason:**
- **Unlikely to be the actual trigger on its own.** The image is purely affectionate with no religious elements.
- **Hypothesis 1:** The reviewer may have flagged the wrong image number, and the actual concern was about the entire pack being submitted alongside religiously-themed packs (boba-milo-5/6 which are Ramadan-themed), causing cross-contamination in review.
- **Hypothesis 2:** The hands-together hugging pose may have been misinterpreted by automated screening as a prayer gesture, though this seems like a stretch.
- **Hypothesis 3:** The pack metadata, description, or tags submitted to LINE may have contained religious references that triggered the flag on image 01.

**Severity:** Low (image content is clearly non-religious)

## Root Cause Analysis

### Category A: Explicitly Religious Packs (boba-milo-5, boba-milo-6)

**Root Cause:** These packs were designed as **Ramadan/Eid al-Fitr themed** sticker packs. The entire concept - from sticker names (marhaban ya ramadan, sahur time, tarawih time, alhamdulillah, doa dulu, selamat idul fitri, mohon maaf lahir batin) to visual elements (mosques, crescent moons, prayer caps, prayer beads, prayer mats, prayer gestures) - is built around Islamic religious practices.

LINE guideline 3.13 explicitly prohibits "images designed to solicit or spread religion, or with strong religious components." These packs have **strong religious components** by design. The rejections are correct applications of the guideline.

**Specific visual triggers found:**
- Mosque silhouettes (boba-milo-5 sticker 08, boba-milo-6 main/tab)
- Crescent moon and stars (Islamic iconography)
- Prayer/salam hand gestures (palms together)
- Prayer caps (kopiah/peci) on characters
- Prayer beads (tasbih)
- Prayer mat (sajadah)
- Islamic religious text: "Mohon maaf lahir batin", "Waktunya Berbuka", "Makan bareng yuk" (in Ramadan context)

**Why only specific images were flagged (not all):** LINE reviewers typically flag the most obvious violations as examples. The rejection of image 08 (boba-milo-5) and images main/tab/07 (boba-milo-6) doesn't mean other stickers are acceptable - it means these were cited as the clearest examples. The entire pack would need revision.

### Category B: Non-Religious Packs with Unclear Rejections (boba-milo-3, boba-milo-4)

**Root Cause (Hypotheses - requires further investigation):**

The "Miss you" sticker (image 01) in both packs contains **no visible religious content**. The two otters are hugging with hearts - a purely affectionate scene. Possible explanations:

1. **Pack metadata contamination:** If the LINE submission title, description, or tags for boba-milo-3/4 referenced Ramadan, religious themes, or were associated with the boba-milo-5/6 series in the submission, this could trigger a 3.13 flag regardless of image content.

2. **Account-level review context:** LINE reviewers may have flagged the entire creator account's submissions after seeing the religious content in boba-milo-5/6, applying 3.13 broadly to concurrent submissions.

3. **Automated pre-screening false positive:** The hugging pose (hands together around each other) could theoretically trigger an automated system looking for "prayer" gestures, though this seems unlikely given how common hugging stickers are.

4. **Reviewer error:** The reviewer may have made a mistake or applied the guideline too broadly across multiple submissions from the same creator.

**Most likely explanation:** Hypothesis 1 or 2 - the submissions were likely bundled or associated in a way that caused cross-contamination of the religious content flag.

## Lessons Learned

1. **LINE has the strictest content policy** among all target platforms for religious content. Always check LINE guidelines before designing packs intended for LINE distribution.
2. **Religious-themed packs should be designed as multi-platform excluding LINE** from the start, rather than designing for all platforms and being surprised by rejection.
3. **Concurrent submissions may be reviewed together** - do not submit religious and non-religious packs at the same time to avoid association.
4. **Reviewers flag example images, not all violations** - one image being rejected means the entire pack concept may be problematic.
