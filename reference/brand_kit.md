# Kawaii Sticker Brand Kit

## Character: Mochi
A small, round kawaii cat character with expressive emotions.

### Visual Specs
- **Species**: Round cat (simplified, chibi proportions)
- **Body Color**: `#FFB6C1` (soft pink) solid flat fill
- **Cheek Blush**: Two circular `#FF69B4` (hot pink) marks on cheeks
- **Outline**: `#333333` (dark gray), thick uniform 3-4px width
- **Eyes**: Simple black oval eyes, no pupils, highly expressive
- **Proportions**: Chibi style - head is 60% of total body height, stubby short limbs
- **Signature Accessory**: Tiny gold star pin on left ear (always present)

### Color Palette
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Soft Pink | `#FFB6C1` | Body fill |
| Hot Pink | `#FF69B4` | Cheek blush |
| Dark Gray | `#333333` | Outlines |
| Gold | `#FFD700` | Star pin accessory |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Die-cut outline border |

### Art Style Rules
- **Flat colors only** - no gradients, no realistic shading
- **Thick uniform outlines** - consistent weight throughout
- **Die-cut sticker style** - thick white border around entire character
- **No text** inside stickers (better for international sales)
- **Centered composition** with padding on all sides
- **Square format** (1:1 aspect ratio)

### Expression Style Guide
- Blush marks are always **circles**, never ovals
- Emotions shown through: eye shape, mouth, body pose, props
- Tears are always **blue** with a teardrop shape
- Anger shown with: puffed cheeks, steam lines, vein mark
- Love shown with: heart-shaped eyes or floating hearts

## Typography
- Pack titles: Rounded sans-serif (Nunito Bold or similar)
- No text inside stickers

## Pack Naming Convention
- "[Character Name] Emotions Vol. X"
- "[Character Name] Daily Life"
- "[Character Name] Food Adventures"
- "[Character Name] [Season/Holiday] Special"
